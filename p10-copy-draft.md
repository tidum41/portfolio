# Interactive insights for UX research teams
Dialogue runs AI-moderated interviews, and each study gets a chat. I redesigned its answer so a research team can move fast on an insight and still check the people behind it.
Caption: The final answer: a count out of 300, with each side one tap from its people. Illustrative data.

## The chat answered in seconds.  [#problem]
The moment
Priya, a researcher at Hearthside, owes her team a share-out by tomorrow morning. She asks the study chat how shoppers read ‘Arrives by Friday’ and gets a fluent paragraph.
Her real question is how many shoppers stand behind each claim, and prose can’t say. So the brief became a principle: every claim shows how many people hold it.

## A quote plays from its moment, inside the answer  [#clips]
Status: Concept
Hear it
Video is the heaviest thing in the panel, so it loads only on a tap and starts at the quote’s timestamp. Checking a claim never leaves the thread.
Caption: Tapping Mei’s quote plays her interview from 18:27 in place. Illustrative data.

## Charts count people out of their own group  [#data-viz]
Status: Concept
Compare a segment
At 300 interviews a segment can hold nine people, and 67% hides that. I adapted chart templates from IBM Carbon for the AI to pick from, and each one labels N of its group.

## Depth is optional, and the reader opens it  [#levels]
Status: Concept
The turn
Priya’s PM wants the sentence and a skeptical director wants the people. I built two ways to reach each level: a depth picker by the composer, and toggles inside the answer.
I assumed that honing in on the smallest level of detail would be useful, but it might not be feasible or efficient for teams. That changed the goal from showing every level to making each one a tap away, so I kept the toggles.
Caption: The picker works like Dovetail’s Fast and Deep modes and asks for depth up front. The toggles start at the count and go deeper on a tap. Illustrative data.
