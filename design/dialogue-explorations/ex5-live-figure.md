## What it repairs
Every proof on the page is a video, so the reader only watches. One figure the reader can tap shows the UI is real and built in code (ANALYSIS section 4 item 5).

## Default and fallback
The Counts take (`/dialogue/motion/live-side.mp4`, its poster and its existing label) is the default. The live UI is the EvidenceLayer (layer, rise) on the Hearthside 225 and 75. No JavaScript, viewports under 360px and reduced motion (option b) keep the take or its poster.
