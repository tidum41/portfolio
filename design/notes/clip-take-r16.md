## Options (Mudit's call)

1. **Cut the take, keep a still.** Replace the video with the poster frame (Ana's player open, nothing cropped). The caption already states the 14 seconds and the timecodes, which a still carries as well as a video. One video fewer on the page (the Clips take is 8 files: light, dark, phone and dark phone, each with a poster) and nothing is repeated. Cheapest and the one I recommend.
2. **Merge into Every.** Drop Mei from Every and let Clips carry the player beat. Rejected: Every's whole point is that the count scrolls out of view while the player is open, and that needs the 75's people and the count in the same frame as Mei.
3. **Change it so it shows something new.** The Clips section's own claim is that the clip loads only on a tap and starts at the moment the quote was said. A take could show that: tap Ana, a short loading beat (the existing arc loader), then the player starting at 04:10 with the transcript line under her quote highlighted as it plays. This uses components the page already has, but the transcript highlight is not in the shipped stage, so it needs Mudit's OK before I touch it, and it adds about a second.

Open choice: 1, 2 or 3. Recommended: 1.
