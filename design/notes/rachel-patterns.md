# Rachel Chen patterns adopted for Dialogue (round 6)

Studied from rachelchen.tech (1Password, Figma) on Nov 1, 2026. Borrow structure and clarity, never her words. TASTE still wins any conflict (no "priority", About page stays off Rachel).

## Section order we keep
1. Final artifact first (hero answer), then the hidden problem.
2. Problem as a claim heading, with the constraint that makes it hard named nearby.
3. One big turn (Levels: every level at once was too slow).
4. Later sections prove the turn (counts, clips, charts, thin data), uneven pacing.
5. Close on what shipped, what is still concept, and one honest gap.

## Claim-style headings
- Every h2 is a sentence that could be wrong, not a topic label.
- Kickers stay short step names (The moment, The turn, Read the count).
- TOC stays short labels; the claim lives in the h2.

## Short framing
- At most two body sentences before the figure does the talking.
- One caption sentence that says what the figure proves.
- First person owns the decision in the same unit as the heading.

## Rejected options
- Show the option, one conditional benefit, then a one-line reason it lost.
- Prefer a compact index or side-by-side (A explored / B kept) over a long process dump.
- At least one verdict dies on a real constraint (448px panel, video weight, thin evidence).

## What we did not copy
- Her "...but" heading pair more than once (TASTE "not X but Y" budget).
- Competition win / outcome metrics.
- Any wording from her pages.
