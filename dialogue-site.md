## Product surfaces (homepage https://www.dialogueai.com/)
- Positioning: "a research platform that shows what real people want and why." "AI-moderated interviews and surveys at scale, turning real customer conversations into decision-ready insights."
- Two products: Interviews; Surveys ("Quantitative surveys. AI-powered surveys that capture structured feedback at scale.")
- 1 Create a study: "Dialogue generates a complete study in minutes: methodology, audience, and discussion guide."
- 2 Recruit: "Invite your own customers or tap into a vetted global panel of 50M+ ... incentives included, no scheduling." Prolific integration: Dialogue is "a Premier Marketplace Partner on the Prolific platform" (https://www.dialogueai.com/blog/dialogue-prolific-partnership)
- 3 Interview at scale: AI moderator, "simultaneously, in over 30 languages. It probes deeper, follows unexpected threads."
- 4 Get instant insights: "synthesizes everything in real time, surfacing patterns, themes, and the findings that matter most."
- Insights Chat: "Ask questions about your research and instantly surface key insights." Sample prompt shown: "Can you create an executive summary?"
- Stimuli: "images, video, GIFs, Figma files, and live websites."
- "Trained and validated by researchers from OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Airbnb, and more."
- Compliance: SOC 2 Type II, GDPR, HIPAA.
- Stat strip: "50% Time saved per study", "50%+ Lower costs vs. alternatives", "x More studies & interviews" (the multiplier renders as 0 in static HTML, animated value not captured).
- Managed service: Persona quote says Dialogue's "research team scoped it, recruited ..., moderated every conversation, and delivered the synthesis."
- Use cases: Discovery, Concept testing, Usability testing, Voice of the customer. Industries: e-commerce and retail, CPG, consumer tech, healthcare and pharma, financial services. Roles: UX researchers, consumer insights, designers, marketers, PMs and engineers (/solutions/*).
- No public pricing page (/pricing returns 404); sales-led ("Book a demo"). No /integrations page.
- Logos on homepage: Afterpay, Assurant, Bilt, Bleecker Street, Block, Blueberry, Cash App, Discord, Doctronic, Google, Kalshi, Lightspeed, Meta, Nextdoor, Paychex, Persona, Shipt, Suno, TikTok, Tonal, Wayfair.
- Funding: "$6M Seed round, led by Lightspeed Venture Partners" (https://www.dialogueai.com/blog/dialogue-seed-round-announcement, 2025-10-17). Founders built products "at Apple, Snapchat, Twitter, and Reddit". "Research that traditionally took weeks now happens in hours."
- Head of Research hire: Nicole Zeng (previously Kalshi Head of UX Research), https://www.dialogueai.com/blog/head-of-research

## Case studies (verbatim numbers)
- Wayfair https://www.dialogueai.com/blog/wayfair-case-study: "dozens of in-depth, AI-moderated shopper interviews"; "synthesized results in less than two days"; "20 participants... double their typical sample size"; "surfacing insights in 36 hours"; "saved the team an estimated 7 hours of researcher time per study"; Weekly Interviews VoC delivers "behavioral trends, along with supporting video clips and verbatims"; "both researchers and non-researchers". Credits: AI moderation, fast synthesis, clips and verbatims.
- Kalshi https://www.dialogueai.com/blog/kalshi-case-study: "5+ studies with an N=25+ were run by a single researcher within a week"; "In a week's time, we learned about 4 different segments and now we are adding 2 more"; "Could we get a share-out by tomorrow morning, is the bar here"; "stakeholders with varying research maturity"; "AI summaries + pull verbatims (and pre-clipped video reels) when stakeholders want evidence"; "Triage quality continuously"; "reducing internal debate". Quote: "I can ship studies constantly, quality-check quickly, and then share what matters—fast."
- Doctronic https://www.dialogueai.com/blog/doctronic-case-study: "watching videos of over 30 real potential patients ... by the afternoon"; "made messaging decisions that same day"; "Validated "AI Doctor" as the winning terminology"; "Data shows where users drop off; video interviews reveal why"; "empowered the Product Team to act as the researcher"; no in-house research team.
- Suno https://www.dialogueai.com/blog/suno-case-study: "under 48 hours"; "gave our designers the power to run studies directly"; compared Hooks design variations; "directly influencing launch decisions".
- Quill https://www.dialogueai.com/blog/quill-case-study: "several dozen in-depth interviews"; "In just one day"; agency sharing with Fortune 500 clients.
- Block https://www.dialogueai.com/blog/block-case-study: "sensitive financial topics"; "decision-ready insights in hours rather than weeks". Mostly video.
- Coachella (Dialogue's own) https://www.dialogueai.com/blog/coachella-2026: "we interviewed 147 live attendees and remote viewers".
- Persona (homepage testimonial only): "segment-level insights in 2 weeks".
- Nextdoor (homepage): "a dramatic acceleration of how we can gather and synthesize user feedback".

## What customers value, ranked by how often it appears
1. Speed to a share-out (all 7).
2. Evidence non-researchers trust: video clips and verbatims (Wayfair, Kalshi, Doctronic).
3. Comparisons: segments, funnel stages, variants (Kalshi, Wayfair, Suno, Doctronic, Persona).
4. Quality checking at volume (Kalshi; "not at the expense of rigor", Doctronic).
5. Non-researchers running research (Doctronic PMs, Suno designers).
