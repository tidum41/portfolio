Brand research: JOOLA USA site CSS exposes color-secondary-3: #2c3ee0 and lime color-secondary-1: #fef632 with primary ink #1a1a1a (joola.com theme tokens). The 2020 Trinity “J” wordmark is documented on joola.com/about-us; Design Tagebuch notes the international rebrand’s purple/lime pair, while the live US storefront and this B2B prototype use black logo marks on white with blue #2C3EE0. No background pattern — logo alone on a clean brand field.
